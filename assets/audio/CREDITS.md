# Zombie vocal recordings

`growl-1.mp3`, `growl-2.mp3`, `growl-3.mp3` are normalized, mono MP3 conversions of `zombie-1.wav`, `zombie-2.wav`, `zombie-3.wav` from **Zombies Sound Pack** by **artisticdude**, created for Summoning Wars.
